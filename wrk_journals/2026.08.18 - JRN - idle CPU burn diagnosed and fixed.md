# 2026.08.18 — Idle CPU burn: diagnosed and fixed (MDR-BUG-FLUX-00005)

Arthur asked how much CPU mdrdp uses when unfocused and idle, then spotted a live
answer in `top`: an idle `mdrdp temper` at 88% of a core. Three live v0.1.63
sessions at 2560x1440 measured 18–88% while untouched (temper 67% lifetime average
over 1h44m at a 30 fps idle stream; kiln 32%; crucible ~14%).

## Diagnosis

A 5 s `sample` of the temper process put ~90% of main-thread time inside
softbuffer's present: `CA::Render::create_image_by_rendering` →
`CGContextDrawImage` → a vImage 16-bit-LUT colorspace conversion of the entire
14.7 MB buffer, every frame (~22 ms/frame at 1440p). Root causes, all in the
present path — the session/decode threads were healthy:

1. softbuffer's CG backend allocates a fresh zeroed buffer per frame and hands
   CoreAnimation a DeviceRGB CGImage, which CA re-renders on the CPU through
   ColorSync on every present. Cost scales with window area.
2. Every server `Damaged` event requested a redraw regardless of occlusion.
3. Nothing told the server to stop encoding for a window nobody could see.

## Fix (task branch, two commits)

- **Suppress Output** (`98c927c`): `WindowEvent::Occluded` now drives
  `SessionCommand::SetVisibility` → Suppress Output PDU (MS-RDPBCGR 2.2.11.3);
  damage no longer requests redraws while occluded; reveal sends the full
  inclusive desktop rect (which is also the full-repaint request). Proven live:
  suppress/resume lines fired on real occlusion transitions during validation.
- **IOSurface present** (`e9575e8`): new `src/present.rs` — on macOS the frame
  goes to CoreAnimation as an IOSurface (pool of three, never rewriting the one
  on glass), moving colorspace conversion to the GPU compositor. softbuffer stays
  as the portable path, automatic fallback, and `MDRDP_PRESENT=soft` A/B lever.

## Numbers (quench, 1920x1080, Avc444v2, release build, 60 s windows)

| Arm | CPU | Frames | CPU/frame |
|---|---|---|---|
| iosurface + Start-menu stimulus | 1.74 s | 223 | 7.8 ms |
| softbuffer + same stimulus | 2.80 s | 280 | 10.0 ms |
| iosurface, idle, no stimulus | 0.19 s (0.3% of a core) | 14 | — |

The vImage conversion is 74/104 main-thread samples in the softbuffer arm and
absent from the iosurface arm; what remains per frame is decode + `present_into`.
The conversion scales with area, so at Arthur's 2560x1440 the saving is ~2.25x
larger than the 1080p delta suggests (~20 ms/frame there, per the temper sample).

## Surprises / notes

- `top`'s TIME column reads MM:SS — the "68 hours" that triggered this was 68
  minutes. The rate (~2/3 of a core, idle) was real.
- Idle frame rate is content-driven and differs per host: temper idled at 30 fps
  (its cap) while quench idles at ~0.2 fps. Any idle-CPU figure must name the
  host and its idle frame rate.
- CA may short-circuit `setContents` naming the object it already shows, so the
  presenter never rewrites the surface currently on glass — the pool exists for
  correctness, not just tearing.
- System Events cannot see the detached mdrdp process (`unix id` lookup fails),
  so occlusion could not be toggled programmatically; the suppress path was
  proven by the window's natural occlusion transitions during the first run.
- Keychain reads from a fresh task-branch binary get "User canceled" — the
  known recipe is `security find-generic-password -w | mdrdp --password-stdin`.
- Quench rig: my ano connects took the console session; ran `schtasks /run /tn
  mdrdp-prep` via a scripted session afterwards (attempted; completion not
  independently verified — noted on the board).
