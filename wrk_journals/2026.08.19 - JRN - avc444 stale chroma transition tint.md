# AVC444 transition tint (MDR-BUG-FLUX-00010) — stale-chroma flat paint

**What happened.** Hours after MDR-BUG-FLUX-00007 landed (luma pass preserves
delivered chroma, v0.1.69), Arthur watched the repro scene live on temper and
reported two residuals: a one-frame blue tinge where the bar head retreats, and —
wider — window restores drawing their contents with wrong colours until the chroma
catches up. Both are the same mechanism: a content change shipped as a luma-only
frame leaves the preserved odd chroma one catch-up behind, and reconstruction
`4*new_avg - 3*stale` overshoots into hues never on screen. The FLUX-00007 fix
*introduced* this gap-frame regression as the price of fixing the steady state;
this task closed the gap frame too.

**Fix.** `chroma_stale` per-block bitset in `Yuv444Buffer`: the luma pass marks a
chroma'd block whose delivered average jumps more than `STALE_AVG_DELTA` (10);
chroma passes clear covered blocks; paint renders stale blocks from their flat
even/even average until the catch-up. Delivered samples are never destroyed, so
the fallback is purely a paint-time decision that self-heals. Fix commit
0345af8aa8fe33d4cdcbe98879a1a1ddb5ba2c85.

**How the threshold was sized — the part worth reusing.** Extended
`examples/avcreplay.rs` to print, per luma-carrying frame, the distribution of
per-block average deltas between the incoming main frame and the stored even/even
values. Over the 32 captured temper payloads: re-encode noise on unchanged
content tops out at **6**; genuine content change starts at **41**. Threshold 10
sits in an empty gap — no tuning, no false positives in the whole capture.

**How wrongness was measured.** A naive "B spikes above both neighbours" metric
went *up* after the fix (2,271 -> 3,603) because it counts the deliberate
one-frame flat-average softening as well as the overshoot — a reminder that a
metric must encode the failure, not the change. The decisive metric: pixels
blue-dominant (B > R + 20) for exactly one frame on content yellow-dominant on
both neighbours — a hue no flat average of the block's real colours can produce.
Pre-fix 1,706 (1,656 in the retreating-bar frame Arthur saw, 10-pixel bursts at
every head jump); post-fix **0**. The window-restore analogue shows in the
first-paint frames: mean B 71 pre, 25 post, against a true post-chroma 8.4 (the
4:2:0 floor no client can beat during the gap).

**Rejected designs.** Invalidating `chroma_seen` on luma change (stuck-forever:
if chroma genuinely didn't change the server never re-sends it, and the discarded
samples were valid); clamping reconstruction harder (kills the single-pixel
colour detail 4:4:4 exists to deliver — a red pixel on white has exactly the
overshoot signature).

**Validation.** Both regression tests proven red with the fix disabled (the
stale-paint test fails with `[255,23,255]` vs `[73,120,68]` — the overshoot in
person). Crate suite 213, workspace 577+13+5, oracle 10/10, clippy/fmt,
check-windows.sh all green. No live visual pass yet: temper reachable again for
Arthur but a same-account connect would take over his session; his next ordinary
session is the visual check.
