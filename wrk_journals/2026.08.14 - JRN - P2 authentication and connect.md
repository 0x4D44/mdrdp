## Outcome

mdrdp authenticates to `temper` and reaches capability exchange from a release build.
The largest risk in the part is retired: **sspi's CredSSP works against a Microsoft
Account**. FreeRDP succeeding in P1b was no evidence for this — the two use different
NTLM implementations — and it could genuinely have gone the other way.

## Corrections to things I published wrongly

Recorded because both were stated to Arthur as fact before being checked, and both were
caught by a Gauntlet critic rather than by me.

**1. "CredSSP is 119 ms — 84% of connect time" was wrong.** The code had a single stage
covering everything after TLS, so it could not isolate CredSSP at all; 119 ms was the
whole post-TLS sequence. With per-stage capture, CredSSP's median is **6.5 ms** and the
dominant connector stage is **ConnectionFinalization at ~29 ms median**. I would have
carried that inverted picture straight into P2b's reconnect budget.

**2. Single-sample timings presented as figures.** "142 ms total" was one run. Across
eight consecutive runs the total ranges 60.9–238.4 ms, median 129.0. This is the *second*
time in this project I have quoted a single sample as a settled number — the first was
the 4.20 ms latency floor. Now recorded in `lessons_learnt.md` as a pattern rather than
two incidents.

**3. The stated reason for including finalization was false.** The HLD claimed Save
Session Info carries the auto-reconnect cookie P2b needs. `ConnectionResult` exposes no
such field. Finalization stays in scope because the connection is unusable without it,
and capturing the reconnect cookie is now an open question owned by P2b.

## The licensing question, settled from source

The pass condition asks the trace to show licensing as a leg, and it never appeared. The
answer is not instrumentation: when a **message channel is present**, IronRDP reads the
first licensing PDU during `SecureSettingsExchange` and advances licensing inline via
`advance_licensing_exchange`, never entering the `LicensingExchange` state
(`ironrdp-connector-0.10.0/src/connection.rs`). `temper` has a message channel —
`ConnectTimeAutoDetection` in our trace runs on it. Licensing happens; it is simply not a
distinct state on this host.

## Decisions worth keeping

- **`ironrdp-tls` is unusable as shipped.** Its rustls backend accepts every certificate
  (`NoCertificateVerification`). Replaced with trust-on-first-use pinning; only the chain
  check is replaced, TLS signature verification still runs the provider's real algorithms.
- **Blocking I/O, not async.** One TCP connection; multiplexing is at the RDP channel
  layer. An executor adds a scheduling hop between byte and frame for no concurrency we
  need.
- **Stage capture reads upstream's own reporting** rather than reimplementing the
  connector loop. Allowlists one field, so it cannot leak session data by construction.

## A product finding that arrived sideways

macOS keychain ACLs bind to the exact binary, so every `cargo build` invalidated the
grant and re-prompted. Worked around with `-A` on the single item for development.

The real fix is **code signing with a stable identity**, and that turns out to be a
functional requirement rather than a distribution chore: P7's favourites launcher spawns
a process per session, so without a stable signature every launch prompts the user for
their login password. Worth knowing before P7 rather than during it.
