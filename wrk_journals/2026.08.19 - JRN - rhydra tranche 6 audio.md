# 2026.08.19 — JRN — rhydra tranche 6 (audio)

Parked in the understand phase, before any HLD, on a host fact.

## What happened

The tranche's first question was the cheap decisive one: can rhydra capture what
quench is playing? WASAPI loopback attaches to the default **render** endpoint, and
a headless box may not have one.

quench does not. Six render endpoints, all not-present or unplugged, zero active,
`GetDefaultAudioEndpoint` returning `0x80070490`. Full detail and the four options
in `wrk_docs/2026.08.19 - PARK - tranche 6 audio needs a render endpoint on the
host.md`.

## Why it is worth a document rather than a line

Every cheap check said the host was fine. `Win32_SoundDevice` reports the Realtek
adapter with `Status: OK`; `Audiosrv` and `AudioEndpointBuilder` are both Running.
The registry's `MMDevices\Audio\Render` states were the first hint something was
wrong, and even those needed decoding — `0x20000004` and `0x8` are not-present and
unplugged, not variations on "present". Only WASAPI answers the question directly.

Had I taken the WMI answer, the tranche would have been designed, built and
half-validated before the silence turned up, and the natural reading of silence in
an audio feature is a bug in the code you just wrote.

## The judgement call

I did not build the portable half — the priority sender, the wire message, the Mac
playback path — even though it compiles and tests without a host. Two reasons.
Half a feature that cannot be validated end to end is a checkpoint, not a delivery.
And the capture side's real behaviour (mix format, buffer period, how it reports
silence) is exactly what should shape the wire format and the jitter policy; deriving
those from an assumption I cannot test is how a wire format ends up needing a break.

The design work is not lost — the four banked decisions (PCM16 over Opus, the FIFO
that must not reuse `Slot`, the ssh-tunnel caveat on priority separation, clock
drift as the soak risk) are written down in the park document.

## One thing the park document changes regardless of Arthur's answer

Whatever provides the endpoint, the health ladder should report its absence. Audio
that is silently unavailable is requirement 4 (no visibility) in miniature: the user
cannot tell a missing endpoint from a broken feature. That rung is worth adding even
under the descope option.
