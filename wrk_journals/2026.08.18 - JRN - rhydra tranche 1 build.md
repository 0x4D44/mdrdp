returned only the connect keyframe. Root cause — device removal is ASYNC. The
agent's orphan sweep killed the rig's creator, the old display lingered "present"

forever. The wedge post-mortem's stale-section pattern, reproduced inside our own
bring-up within the hour of designing against it. Fix: AgentOps reports device
IDENTITY (GDI name), and the reconciler restarts the server on a swap or blink
(704b811, two mutation-proven tests).

drain smoke 133 frames + 61 diff-rects from 60 keystrokes; taskkill'd server

processes; task disabled; rig creator + 240 Hz restored for tonight's queue.
