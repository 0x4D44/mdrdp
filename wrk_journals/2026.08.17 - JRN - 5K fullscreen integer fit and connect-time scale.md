# 2026.08.17 — 5K fullscreen: integer fit past the H.264 ceiling, scale at connect

Arthur reported soft text at full-screen. Root cause chain on his two 5K monitors
(5120x2880): fullscreen asked for native, `clamp_to_encodable` shrank it to
4096x2304 at 160% (the level 5.2 ceiling), and `present_into` stretched that
1.25x with nearest-neighbour — every fourth pixel row/column duplicated, glyph
stems alternating 1px/2px. Two secondary contributors: 160% is fractional DPI
(Windows renders it softer than 100/200), and the session connected at scale 0
then switched DPI mid-session, which DWM answers by bitmap-stretching every
non-per-monitor-DPI-aware remote window.

Shipped in 0.1.49 (commit 99cb9e7):

- `session::fullscreen_request` — one shared decision for the fullscreen toggle
  and the connect path. Under the ceiling: native, 1:1, true Retina. Past it:
  smallest integer division (5K → 2560x1440 at 100%), so the presenter's 2x
  stretch is uniform pixel-doubling. The fractional clamp stays reachable via
  Settings ▸ Graphics ▸ "Integer scaling on 5K+ displays" (off) for A/B.
- Connect-time plan: a session that will open fullscreen now connects AT the
  planned resolution and advertises the scale in the GCC core data (new
  `display` module — CoreGraphics probe on macOS, `None` elsewhere; winit
  cannot enumerate monitors before its loop runs, and the loop's one Resumed
  is what creates the session window, so winit was not an option).
- `service_resize` now drops a resize naming the state the session is already
  in (`resize_is_redundant`), so the startup renegotiation after a planned
  connect costs nothing instead of a Deactivate All round.

Live evidence (quench, `ano`, 20 s, graceful): connected directly at
2560x1440, no "resolution: requested" line (guard fired), AVC444v2, 33 frames,
0 decode errors, screenshot clean at 2560x1440.

Known behaviour change: a fullscreen-at-start session that is toggled OUT of
fullscreen now gets a 2560x1440 windowed session (the connect size) rather than
the 1920x1080 default it used to connect at.

Open question, deliberately left to a live A/B: whether filtered-fractional
4096x2304 (more detail, mild softness, needs a real resampling filter we do not
have yet) would beat integer 2560x1440 (less detail, perfectly uniform) for
text. The Settings toggle exists so the comparison is one click, but the
fractional side still presents through nearest-neighbour today.
