## Supporting: what the cheap checks said instead

Both hosts look healthy by every check short of asking WASAPI. On quench:

```
Win32_SoundDevice : Realtek High Definition Audio, Status: OK
Audiosrv          : Running
AudioEndpointBuilder : Running
Get-PnpDevice -Class MEDIA : Realtek(R) Audio = OK
```

That gap is the finding: WMI reports the adapter, not whether any endpoint is
active. Ask WASAPI.
